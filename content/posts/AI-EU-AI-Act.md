"The EU AI Act divides AI Systems into 5 different risk categories

Minimal Risk: Models used in video games and spam filters.
Limited Risk: Models that produce content such as text or images.
High Risk: Models that can negatively affect the health, safety or the fundamental rights of persons. These include systems used in health, education, recruitment, critical infrastructure management, law enforcement or justice.
Unacceptable Risk: Models that use social scoring, predictive policing, real-time identification systems or any other manipulative practices.
General-purpose AI: Models that are generic enough to perform a wide range of tasks"
